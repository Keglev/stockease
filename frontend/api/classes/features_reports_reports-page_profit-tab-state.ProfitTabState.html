    Class ProfitTabState

    The profit tab's state: two tables over one period, their charts, and their exports.

    Remarks

    Provided by the reports page and scoped to it (ADR 039), which keeps the lifetime the state had as a page member while giving the tab a name of its own. Two tables rather than one because the tab is one question - what the business earned - asked by product and by supplier over the same window; splitting them would put one period signal in two owners.

    Members carry no `profit` prefix: the collaborator is the namespace, so the page reads `profit.rows()` where it used to read `profitRows()`.
