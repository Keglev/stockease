    Class StockTabState

    The stock tab's state: what the business holds, its totals strip, its chart and its export.

    Remarks

    Provided by the reports page and scoped to it (ADR 039). The simplest of the tab states and deliberately converted alongside the profit tab rather than after it: between them the two exercise every part of the collaborator boundary - a period and no period, a derived sort and an in-place one, a filtered export and a sorted one - which is what makes them a proof of the pattern rather than one example of it.

    Members carry no `stock` prefix: the collaborator is the namespace.

    Readonlytotals

    totals: Signal<{ products: number; units: number; value: number } | null> = ...

    The stock tab's headline figures, summed from the rows the tab already loaded.

    Derived rather than fetched, so a single source answers both halves of the tab: a strip and the table beneath it cannot disagree when the strip is the table added up. The unfiltered rows on purpose - the strip states what the business holds, which a text box must not appear to change.
