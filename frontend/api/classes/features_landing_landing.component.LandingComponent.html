    screenshots: Signal<

    > = ...

    The four screenshots, re-pointed whenever the visitor changes language or theme.

    Sixteen assets exist under a strict naming contract - `--.png` for the four pages, both languages and both schemes - and four are on screen at a time, one staged in the hero and three in the gallery further down. Recomputing the paths from the page's own toggles is the entire point: a landing page can claim it is translated and themed, or it can change in front of the visitor when they press the toggles, and the second is the only one that proves anything.
