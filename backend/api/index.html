StockEase API (2.12.0)

Download OpenAPI specification:

URL: https://github.com/Keglev License: MIT

StockEase is an inventory management system built around stock movements as the single source of truth for quantity. The API covers the product catalogue, the two counterparty registers (suppliers and customers), the invoice lifecycle, stock movements and returns, a reporting read model, and the product change log.

Response envelope

Most single-resource and mutation responses use the ApiResponse envelope (success, message, data). List endpoints return the bare array, and creation endpoints return the bare created record. Every error produced by the shared exception handler uses the envelope with success: false.

Authentication and roles

All endpoints except POST /api/auth/login and GET /health require a JWT bearer token. Authorization is declared per endpoint: reads and most writes accept ROLE_USER or ROLE_ADMIN, while destructive or lifecycle-critical operations (product create and delete, supplier, customer and invoice deletion, invoice close and payment) require ROLE_ADMIN. A request without a usable token receives 401 with the envelope; an authenticated request lacking the role receives 403.

Domain doctrine expressed by the API

Several rules are enforced at the API surface rather than left to convention:

  • Purchase and sale movements exist only through invoice closing. They cannot be recorded directly; closing an invoice books them as a side effect.
  • POST /api/stock-movements accepts two reasons only - LOST and DESTROYED. It is a loss ledger: stock never arrives through it. Any other reason is rejected with 400 before the service is reached.
  • Returns are a first-class operation with their own endpoint, POST /api/returns. It records the movement and raises the invoice line's returned quantity in one transaction.
  • Customer master data has no update endpoint by design. Customers can be listed, fetched, created and soft-deleted; correcting customer details is out of scope for this API.
  • Product quantity cannot be assigned directly. It changes only as a consequence of movements.

Health

Database-backed liveness probe

Health check endpoint

Database-backed liveness probe used by uptime monitors and orchestration. Probes the database with a trivial query and reports combined application and database liveness as JSON. A failing probe returns 503 rather than 500: it signals a transient dependency outage, not an application bug. No authentication required. Used by:

  • Kubernetes (liveness/readiness probes)
  • Docker (healthcheck instruction)
  • Load balancers (backend health verification)
  • Monitoring systems (Prometheus, DataDog, etc.)

Creates a product from a name, an operator-assigned SKU and a purchase price. totalValue is derived for the response rather than stored.

Creation is master-data maintenance and accepts no quantity: the product starts at zero stock, and every unit it later holds arrives the only way stock ever does - by closing a purchase invoice.

Get products with critically low stock

Returns products whose quantity is below the reorder threshold of 5 units, scoped to products that have ever been stocked. A product sold down to zero stays listed; a product that has never been purchased is treated as new rather than low and never appears, whatever its quantity. When every product is adequately stocked the endpoint returns a bare status message object instead of an empty array.

Create an invoice with its lines

Creates an invoice together with all of its lines, in status OPEN. Creation records the invoice but books no stock - that happens when the invoice is closed.

Which counterparty the type requires is validated in the service, so a mismatch (a purchase without a supplier, a sale naming one) returns 400 with the plain envelope rather than the field-error map.

Stock Movements

Standalone stock corrections; purchases and sales are booked by closing invoices

Record a standalone stock correction

Records a movement that stands on its own and applies its quantity change to the product atomically. Movements are append-only: a recorded movement is never updated or deleted.

Only two reasons are accepted here - LOST and DESTROYED. This endpoint is a loss ledger: stock never enters through it. The other reasons are rejected at the API surface before the service is reached, because they belong to other flows: PURCHASE and SOLD come into existence only by closing an invoice, and the two return reasons go through POST /api/returns.

Both reasons require a remark naming what happened to the stock, and neither accepts a price: a cost snapshot belongs to a purchase line, and purchases are not recorded here.

Returns

Returns against an invoice line, in either direction

Return units against an invoice line

Records a return in one transaction: it writes the stock movement and raises the invoice line's returned quantity together, so the two can never drift apart. When every line of an invoice is fully returned, the invoice flips to FULLY_RETURNED.

Accepts the two return directions only - RETURN_FROM_CUSTOMER (units come back into stock from a customer) and RETURNED_TO_SUPPLIER (units leave stock back to a supplier). Any other reason is rejected before the service is reached.

The endpoint lives under the movement module because the movement service is what orchestrates the return, calling into the invoice module rather than the other way around.

productId is required even though the invoice line already determines it: stating it lets the service's item-product coherence check catch a client that referenced the wrong line.
